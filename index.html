Why Choose a Licensed Tourist Guide?

Hiring a licensed tourist guide ensures you receive accurate and knowledgeable commentary during your visit. Licensed guides in Greece are accredited by the Ministry of Tourism and are legally allowed to lead tours inside archaeological sites, museums, and historical landmarks. We carry an official badge with our ID number.
